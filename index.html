Joanna Luc

I am a philosopher of physics currently employed at Jagiellonian University. My research interest include topics in the philosophy of physics (foundations of General Relativity and Quantum Mechanics, symmetries, determinism/indeterminism, modal notions in physics) as well more general philosophical issues (especially laws of nature and their relation to other metaphysical notions). I have obtained PhD degree in 2020 at Jagiellonian University and before that I have obtained a BA and an MA in philosophy as well as BSc and MSc in physics at the same university; I also did MPhil in History and Philosophy of Science and Medicine at Cambridge University.
